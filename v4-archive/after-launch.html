After launch

Launch is just the beginning.

Every project includes a structured handover, a short post-launch support period and guidance to move forward confidently.

Deployed to a suitable platform, with domain connection and launch setup where your scope includes it. Domains, hosting subscriptions, premium plugins and other third-party services are paid separately unless your proposal says otherwise.

14 days of support

Every project includes 14 days of post-launch support for bugs in the delivered work. New features, redesigns, extra integrations and third-party issues are handled separately.

Training and handover

You get your access details and basic guidance on managing what the project covers: content updates, enquiries, submissions or your CRM. Extended training can be arranged separately.

Ongoing maintenance

Monthly maintenance from $75 per month. Price depends on platform, complexity and support level. May include health checks, supported updates, backups where available, minor content changes and basic troubleshooting.

Continued development

Extra pages, new features, CRM integrations, funnel improvements and conversion optimisation can be commissioned separately as you grow.

My commitment
