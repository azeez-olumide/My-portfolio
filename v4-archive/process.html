How I work

From first conversation to launch.

A clear, collaborative process that keeps things organised and avoids surprises.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    1 to 2 business days

    We talk about your business, goals, audience, features, budget and timeline, then define the scope.

  2. 02

    Proposal & kickoff

    1 to 3 business days

    You get a tailored proposal with deliverables, price, timeline and payment terms. Work starts once it is approved and the first payment is in.

  3. 03

    Structure & design

    3 to 7 business days

    I plan the page structure, user journey and visual direction. You review and give feedback before development.

  4. 04

    Development

    3 to 15+ business days

    The approved design becomes responsive pages, forms, integrations and the features in scope.

  5. 05

    Testing & revisions

    2 to 5 business days

    I test across screen sizes, check forms and integrations, and fix issues in scope. You get revision rounds to refine the result.

  6. 06

    Launch & handover

    1 to 2 business days

    Final payment, launch, domain connection where applicable, final checks, and a handover with access details and guidance.
