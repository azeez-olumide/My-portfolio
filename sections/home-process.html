Process

No unnecessary complexity. No unclear expectations. Just a structured process from the first conversation to launch.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    1 to 2 business days

    We talk through your business, goals, audience, required features, budget and timeline, so the scope is defined before anything is built.

  2. 02

    Strategy and structure

    1 to 3 business days

    You receive a tailored proposal. Once it is approved, I plan the page structure and user journey around what the site needs to achieve.

  3. 03

    Design

    3 to 7 business days

    I design the visual direction and layouts. You review and give feedback before any development starts.

  4. 04

    Development

    3 to 15+ business days

    The approved design becomes responsive pages, forms, integrations and the features included in the agreed scope.

  5. 05

    Testing and launch

    2 to 5 business days

    I test across screen sizes, check forms and integrations, and fix issues within scope. Then I configure the launch and connect your domain where applicable.

  6. 06

    Handover and support

    1 to 2 business days

    You get the access details and guidance for what the project covers, plus 14 days of post-launch support for in-scope bugs.
