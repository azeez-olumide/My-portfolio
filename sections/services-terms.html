Payment terms

Standard projects: 50% / 50%

Landing pages, business websites and standard funnels. 50% upfront reserves your slot and starts the work. 50% is due before final launch or handover.

Web applications: milestones

Typically 40% upfront, 30% after the core design and development milestone is approved, and 30% before final deployment and handover. Confirmed in your proposal.

Monthly maintenance

Billed monthly in advance. Scope, service period and cancellation terms are set out in the service agreement.

Good to know

Work begins after the initial payment and receipt of required materials. Quotes are valid for 14 days. Work outside the agreed scope is quoted separately. Final terms are confirmed in your individual proposal.

After launch

14 days of post-launch support

Covers bugs in the work I delivered, within the agreed scope. New features, redesigns and issues caused by third-party services are handled separately.

Two revision rounds included

Two rounds of revisions are included within the agreed scope, so feedback is organised and the project stays on schedule.

Hosting and domain

I deploy your site and connect your domain where the scope includes it. Domain registration, hosting subscriptions, premium software and third-party fees are paid separately unless agreed otherwise.

Handover and ongoing care

You receive access details and guidance for what the project covers. Monthly maintenance and further development can be added whenever you need them.
