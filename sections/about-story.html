The short version

I'm Azeez, a web designer, developer and Meta Ads specialist who started in graphic design.

My journey into digital design and development began with graphic design, where I learned how visual communication shapes the way people understand a brand.

That curiosity moved beyond static visuals. I taught myself Python and built a calculator and a ping-pong game, which introduced me to programming logic and interactive experiences. From there I moved into web development and Figma-based interface design.

Discovering GoHighLevel changed how I think about the work. Websites, landing pages, funnels, CRM and automation can work together to support a business's whole customer journey.

Alongside building websites, I have spent roughly 3 to 4 years working with Meta Ads, planning campaign strategy and managing campaigns, including the day-to-day work of running ads. That gives me both sides of digital marketing: the creative that earns attention and the campaign thinking that turns it into enquiries. Today I bring design, development and advertising together, and I work as an independent professional with clients in Nigeria and abroad.

How I approach the work

  • Design with intention

    Every layout, visual element and interaction should contribute to one coherent experience.

  • Build for functionality

    A site should work reliably on every device and make it easy to navigate and take action.

  • Think beyond the page

    Websites, funnels and connected systems belong to a wider customer journey.

  • Join creative and performance

    Good design gets attention. Good campaign management makes sure that attention reaches the right people.

Thoughtful design. Functional development. Built with purpose.
