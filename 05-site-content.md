## Process: From first conversation to launch

Every project follows a clear, collaborative process designed to keep things organised, minimise surprises and deliver a website or digital product that meets your goals.

1. **Discovery and consultation (1 to 2 business days).** We discuss your business, project goals, target audience, required features, budget and timeline. This helps determine the right solution and define the project scope.
2. **Proposal and project kickoff (1 to 3 business days).** You receive a tailored proposal outlining the deliverables, pricing, timeline and payment terms. Once approved and the initial payment is received, we finalise the requirements and begin.
3. **Structure and design (3 to 7 business days).** I plan the page structure, user journey and visual direction before developing the experience. You review the design and give feedback before we move to implementation.
4. **Development and integration (3 to 15+ business days).** The approved design is brought to life with responsive layouts, functionality, forms, integrations and other features included in the agreed scope.
5. **Testing and revisions (2 to 5 business days).** I test across relevant screen sizes, check functionality, validate forms and integrations, and address issues within the agreed scope. You also have the included revision rounds to refine the final result.
6. **Launch and handover (1 to 2 business days).** Once the final payment is completed, I configure the launch, connect the domain where applicable, perform final checks and hand over the project with relevant access details and guidance.

**Typical project timelines**

| Project type | Estimated duration |
|--------------|--------------------|
| Landing page | 5 to 10 business days |
| Business website | 2 to 3 weeks |
| Sales funnel | 2 to 4 weeks |
| Web application MVP | 4 to 10+ weeks |

**A few things to know**
- Timelines begin once the project is approved, the initial payment is received and the required materials are available.
- Your feedback and approvals help keep the project on schedule.
- Complex functionality, additional integrations or changes to the agreed scope may extend delivery.
- The timeline for your specific project is confirmed in your proposal.

## What happens after launch

Launching your website is just the beginning. Every project includes a structured handover, a short post-launch support period and guidance to help you move forward confidently.

1. **Hosting and domain.** Your website is deployed to an appropriate hosting platform, with domain connection and launch configuration included where specified in your project scope. Domain registration, hosting subscriptions, premium plugins and other third-party services are paid separately by the client unless explicitly included in the proposal.
2. **Post-launch support.** Every project includes **14 days** of post-launch support to address bugs attributable to the delivered work and help resolve issues within the agreed project scope. New features, redesigns, additional integrations and issues caused by third-party services are handled separately.
3. **Training and handover.** You receive the relevant access details and basic guidance on how to manage the parts of your website covered by the project. Depending on the platform and scope, this may include updating content, managing enquiries, reviewing submissions or navigating your CRM. Extended training and additional sessions can be arranged separately.
4. **Ongoing maintenance.** Monthly maintenance is available as an additional service, starting at $75 per month, with the final price depending on platform, complexity, update requirements and level of support. Depending on the plan it can include routine website health checks, software and plugin updates where applicable, basic backups and recovery checks where supported, minor content updates within an agreed allowance, and basic troubleshooting and technical assistance.
5. **Continued development and optimisation.** Additional pages, new functionality, CRM integrations, funnel improvements, performance enhancements and conversion optimisation can be commissioned separately.

**My commitment:** You'll receive a clear handover, transparent expectations and a defined path for getting help after launch, with no hidden assumptions about what's included.
